How Does Tapping a Credit Card Actually Pay?

Listen now
Spotify (opens in a new tab)Apple Podcasts (opens in a new tab)Other apps (opens in a new tab)

You tap your credit card on the machine.

Beep. Green check mark. Done.

But... what actually just happened?

No cash moved. The card barely touched anything.

So how did the shop know the payment was okay?

And why can a card sometimes be declined even when there’s money in the account?

That tiny tap is actually sending a permission request through several computers before the beep ever happens.

Topic: Money & Business · Published 12 September 2026

Talk about it after listening

  1. The card has no battery inside. How does its little chip wake up?
  2. When you hear the beep, has the money moved to the shop yet? What happened instead?
  3. How is the lemonade-stand promise like paying with a card?
