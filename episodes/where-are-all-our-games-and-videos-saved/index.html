Where Are All Our Games and Videos Saved?

Listen now
SpotifyApple PodcastsOther apps

You press play on a YouTube video, and it starts working. Then suddenly, it stops… and a spinning circle appears.

You didn’t press pause. So why did it stop? Isn’t the video already inside your phone?

Somehow, the video is coming from somewhere else, far away. Your phone is asking for tiny pieces and waiting for them to arrive.

Sometimes they arrive fast. Sometimes they don’t. And that spinning circle means your phone is still waiting for the next pieces to come.
