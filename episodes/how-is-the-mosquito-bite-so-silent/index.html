How Is the Mosquito Bite So Silent?

Listen now
SpotifyApple PodcastsOther apps

You’re outside, and suddenly later you see a mosquito bite on your arm.

But wait… when did that happen?

You don’t remember anything poking you.

You didn’t feel it at all.

So how did the mosquito find you?

And how did it poke your skin without you noticing?

And why does the itching only start later?

It turns out the mosquito does something first… before it even starts drinking.
