Why Do Tears Come Out When I Cry?

The lost dog finally comes home, and your eyes fill with tears.

But you're happy. Why are you crying?

Were there tears in your eyes all along?

Where does the extra water come from?

Why does some roll down your cheeks while your nose starts to run?

And why does an onion make tears appear for a completely different reason?

Quick answer: Why Do Tears Come Out When I Cry?

Tears come from a small gland above the outer corner of each eye and drain through tiny holes near the nose, which is why your nose runs when you cry. Tears keep the eye clean and wet, and strong feelings can make the gland produce many more.

Topic: Your Body · Published 8 October 2026

Talk about it after listening

  1. Where is your eye's "faucet" (the tear gland), and where is its "drain"?
  2. Why does your nose get runny when you cry?
  3. Onions make your eyes water too. Is that for the same reason as crying at a happy movie?
