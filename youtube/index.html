Dad, How Does It Work? on YouTube

Dad, How Does It Work? is a science podcast for curious kids ages 5–10. Every full episode is on our YouTube channel @DadHowDoesItWork (opens in a new tab), and a short video explainer goes up every week. Each video is titled with the question kids ask, like “Why is the sky blue?” or “How do planes fly?”.
